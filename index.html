What it does

Analyzes supported FiTech Dashboard and Basic Sensors CSV logs, highlights likely areas to investigate, provides beginner-friendly charts, before/after comparisons, troubleshooting references, vehicle history, and model/handheld-specific procedures where mapped.

Important: diagnostic findings are evidence-based suggestions, not guaranteed mechanical diagnoses.

Safety: Vehicle repair, fuel-system work, electrical work, ignition testing, tuning and road testing can cause vehicle/property damage, fire, injury or death if performed incorrectly. Use is entirely at your own risk. Independently verify all recommendations before acting.

Limited-use authorization

The current free release uses a 60-day, non-transferable authorization tied to one Windows installation. Online activation is designed to issue that authorization automatically.

Feedback

Feedback can be submitted from inside the application. Optional screenshots, FiTech logs, and vehicle/profile information remain under the user's control. Manual support: netwarker@gmail.com.
